It is not investment advice and not a tip service. Always check the chart yourself, decide your own entry and stop, and size every trade so a loss is small.

1. Getting started

  1. Sign in at tradeready.in/login with your Google account. You stay signed in on that device until you tap Sign out (bottom of the left menu on desktop, top-right icon on phone).
  2. Install it like an app (optional). iPhone: open the site in Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen. It then opens full screen like a normal app.
  3. Find your way around. On desktop the menu is on the left. On phone it is the bar at the bottom: Today · Scanner · Index · Search · Market. Most pages have small tabs under the title (for example Scanner → F&O · Equity · Impulse table).
  4. Refresh. Data updates on its own. To force fresh data tap the ⟳ button at the top. If the app looks old after an update, pull down to refresh or close and reopen it.
  5. Tap a stock name anywhere to open its chart on TradingView in a new tab.

Setups use completed candles only: the daily scan looks at yesterday's close until today's candle is finished, and the hourly scan at the last finished hour. That is on purpose, so a signal does not appear and vanish inside a half-formed candle. Prices on the cards are refreshed live during market hours.

2. The 5-minute daily routine

Before 9:15 (or the evening before)

  1. Market weather (Today page). Score 55+ = normal trading. 40 to 55 = trade small and only the best. Under 40 = mostly sit out or protect profits.
  2. Heads-up strip. Avoid stocks in the F&O ban and stocks with results in the next few days.
  3. Strategies page. See which strategies are Open today and which stocks pass all their rules. These are your shortlist.

During and after the market

  1. Scanner → Ready. Open each Ready stock's chart. Prefer grade A, With trend, a Leading or Improving sector and ✓ No 200 DMA / weekly EMA wall.
  2. Wait for the trigger. A setup is Ready before it triggers. Enter only when price confirms (see each strategy's exit line). Put the stop in at once.
  3. Evening. Check Big money (OI), Unusual and the Impulse table for tomorrow's list.
Rule of thumb. The site is built top-down: market → sector → stock → setup → trigger. A good setup in a bad market or a lagging sector fails far more often. If the top layers are weak, trade less.

3. Today: the dashboard

Everything you need before you trade, on one screen. From top to bottom:

Market clock

Shows whether the market is open, the time of the last scan, how many setups are Ready, and a small breadth ribbon: 6 bars, one per session, showing the % of F&O stocks bullish on the Daily chart (green 70%+, orange 40 to 70%, red under 40%).

Heads-up strip

  • New today: setups that turned Ready since the last session. Tap to open the Scanner.
  • F&O ban: stocks whose open interest crossed 95% of the exchange limit (MWPL). No fresh F&O positions are allowed in them; avoid.
  • Results soon: company results within the next days. Expect a big gap either way; most traders avoid holding through results.

Index cards

Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Nifty Midcap 50, Fin Nifty and Nifty Next 50. Each card shows the level, today's % change (marked live 15:30 when it is a live price), the 5-day change (5D), a 1-month chart, and chips:

  • VK chip: the index's VK reading (see VK).
  • OI chip: what futures money did (Long build-up, Short covering, Big positions building and so on).
  • PCR: put-call ratio of the option chain. Chips carry the date of the data, so you always know if a reading is from today or the last session.

Tap a card for the full index page.

Morning headline

A newspaper-style summary: which sectors lead and drag, how many stocks rose and fell, breadth, and how many setups are Ready. Below it, sectors ranked by their 1-day move.

4. Market weather

One score from 0 to 100 that says how friendly the market is for long trades, drawn as weather:

ScoreWeatherHow to trade
70+☀️ Clear skiesBroad strength. Normal size, most setups work.
55 to 69⛅ Partly sunnyOkay. Be selective, favour A grades and leading sectors.
40 to 54🌧 Light showersWeak. Half size, fewer trades, tight stops.
Under 40⛈ Storm warningHostile. Mostly cash; protect what you have.

The six parts (tap the card to see them):

  • Daily breadth: % of F&O stocks bullish on the Daily chart. Short-term mood.
  • Weekly breadth: % bullish on the Weekly chart. The real health of the market; under 40% is weak.
  • Nifty trend: Nifty's Weekly and Daily state.
  • Sectors: how many of 20 sectors are Leading or Improving.
  • Money flow: % of F&O stocks with long build-up or short covering.
  • VIX: India VIX, the fear gauge. Lower VIX scores higher.

Market line (shown under the parts and used by the strategies):

  • Turning up: daily breadth rose by several points over 3 sessions and Nifty's Daily is not bearish. Best time to buy dips.
  • No clear turn: nothing decisive. Strategies run at half size or wait.
  • Falling / Still falling: breadth dropping fast or Nifty Daily bearish. Most buy strategies close.
  • Overheated: daily breadth 85%+. Rallies are stretched; expect a cooldown.

5. The 7 strategies

Today → Strategies. Each strategy is a fixed set of rules. The page shows whether it is Open today (the market allows it), which stocks pass every rule, and which are close ("Dipping now, next to turn"). Use them as complete checklists: if a stock is not listed, it failed at least one rule.

Gate = the market condition the strategy needs. "Open" means allowed today; "half size" means allowed with smaller positions; "Closed" means skip it today.

1 · Sector Leader Swing

F&O · 3 to 5 days

Idea: buy a short hourly dip in a strong stock inside a strong sector.

  • Market Turning up with daily breadth under 85% (full size), or No clear turn (half size)
  • Swing Pullback setup, stage Ready, grade A, With trend
  • Not extended: under 4 ranges (ATR) above the 50 EMA
  • Sector Leading or Improving, its index Daily bullish, 50%+ of its stocks weekly bullish

Entry and exit: enter on an hourly close above the entry level. Book half at 2R, trail the rest under hourly swing lows, exit by day 5 if it has not worked.

2 · Cooldown Buy

F&O and Equity · 5 to 10 days

Idea: buy strong stocks on their daily pullback; best right after an overheated rally cools down.

  • Market not Still falling, and Nifty Daily not Bearish
  • Full size when a cooldown is under way (daily breadth was 85%+ in the last 6 sessions and has cooled to 60 to 78%), otherwise half
  • Trend Pullback or Daily Overspill, stage Ready, grade A
  • Under 2 ranges above the 50 EMA (near value); sector not Lagging

Entry and exit: daily close above the entry level. Half at 2R, trail the rest under the 20 EMA.

3 · Squeeze Coil

F&O and Equity · 3 to 10 days

Idea: catch a coiled stock as momentum turns up, before the breakout.

  • Market No clear turn, or Turning up with daily breadth under 70%
  • Squeeze Break setup, stage Ready, grade A or B; sector Leading or Improving
  • Bonus: old label 🏆 Prime Setup, or Unusual page shows Buying

Entry and exit: daily close above the 5-day high, ideally on above-normal volume. Half at 2R; exit when the Daily turns Topping.

4 · Weekly Overspill Positional

F&O and Equity · 2 to 4 weeks

Idea: buy a long uptrend when the weekly chart dips into its 20 to 50 EMA zone and the Daily turns up.

  • Market anything except Still falling
  • Weekly Overspill, stage Ready, grade A, With trend; sector not Lagging
  • Bonus (Equity): delivery higher than before

Entry and exit ("accordion"): one third now, one third on a daily close above the entry level, the last third when Weekly momentum turns up. The stop is wide, so size the first lot to cost only your normal risk. Trail under the weekly 20 EMA.

5 · Unusual Activity Confluence

F&O and Equity · 3 to 5 days

Idea: big money showing up in a stock that is already on a setup.

  • Market anything except Still falling
  • Unusual page: bias Buying, score 4 or more
  • Also on a setup, stage Watch or Ready, grade not Warning
  • Weekly Bullish or Impulse; sector Leading or Improving

Entry: when the setup is Ready, on its entry level. Watch-stage names are listed so you can set price alerts.

6 · Equity Base Breakout

Equity · 8 to 10 days

Idea: buy a midcap or smallcap breaking out of a tight base on volume.

  • Equity market Turning up with daily breadth under 85%
  • Breakout page: Ready (close to the level), grade A
  • RS 80 or more, base 20% deep or less, volume inside the base below usual
  • Sector Leading or Improving (or not classified)

Entry and exit: daily close above the breakout level on volume at least 1.5× usual; no volume, no trade. Stop about one daily range below the level. Exit if it closes back inside the base within 3 days.

7 · Breakout + Trend Overspill

F&O · 3 to 7 days

Idea: after a range breakout, buy the first hourly dips into the 20 to 50 EMA zone of the new trend.

  • Market anything except Still falling (half size when Overheated)
  • A range breakout in the last 15 sessions that still holds (price above the old ceiling)
  • Weekly or Daily in Impulse; Hourly Overspill, stage Ready, dip quality A
  • Not extended (under 4 ranges above the Daily 50 EMA); sector not Lagging

Entry and exit: hourly close above the hourly 20 EMA. Stop under the spill low. Half at 2R, trail the rest under the hourly 50 EMA. Best on the first or second dip after the breakout.

A list

Today → A list: every grade-A setup across F&O and Equity on one page, with stage filters (Ready / Triggered / Watch) and the Clear road only filter.

6. Scanner and setup cards

Tabs: F&O (about 218 futures stocks) · Equity (cash stocks outside F&O) · Impulse table.
Setup filter: All · Squeeze · Swing · Trend · Overspill · Patterns · VK (Equity also has Breakout).
Stage filter: Ready · Watch · Triggered, with counts.
Clear road only: hides stocks that have the 200 DMA, 20-week EMA or 50-week EMA within 4% above the price. Those levels often act as a ceiling, so stocks with a clear road have more room to run.

Reading a setup card

  • Symbol and price, plus a live price during market hours. Top right: the grade badge (A, B, C or ! for Warning).
  • Wall chip: ✓ No 200 DMA / weekly EMA wall or ⚠ 50W EMA 2.2% above (resistance close overhead).
  • Setup · Stage · Trend: e.g. Swing Pullback · Ready · With trend.
  • VK chip and sector · sector stage.
  • M · W · D · H chips: Monthly, Weekly, Daily, Hourly state (see states).
  • Detail line: why it qualified, e.g. "Pullback 26% · Volume 0.7x in pullback · OI +113% in up-leg". Volume under 1x in the pullback means sellers are quiet (good).

Stages

StageMeaningWhat to do
WatchThe pattern is forming but momentum has not turned yet.Add to your list, set alerts. Do not buy yet.
ReadyMomentum is turning; the trigger level is close.Plan the trade: entry above the trigger, stop below the pullback low.
TriggeredPrice already crossed the trigger.Only enter if it is still near the entry; do not chase.
RunningNo setup; the stock is in a Daily impulse.Hold if you own it; wait for the next pullback.
StretchedMore than 3 ATR above the 20 EMA.Too far from value; risky to buy, consider booking some.
FadingDaily momentum falling 3 bars in a row while Weekly is still up.A pause or pullback is likely; tighten stops.

Grades

Graded on the last close using volume and futures open interest:

  • A: both checks pass: volume dried up in the pullback and OI rose in the up-move without short build-up in the dip.
  • B: one of the two passes.
  • C: neither passes.
  • ! Warning: short build-up during the pullback (price down, OI up 3%+). Sellers are pressing; avoid.

Trend tag (from the Monthly chart)

  • With trend: Monthly is Bullish or Topping. The big trend is up. Best.
  • Early turn: Monthly is Bottoming. A new uptrend may be starting; smaller size.
  • Counter-trend: Monthly is Bearish. Buying against the big trend; quick trades only.

Old labels (from the original TrendFire)

  • 🏆 Prime Setup: Daily momentum below zero but rising, with a squeeze on. Coiled and turning.
  • ✅ Confirmed Bullish: Daily momentum above zero and rising.
  • ⏳ Building Momentum: Daily momentum below zero but rising.
  • 🎯 Consolidating / ⚠️ Extended: the Hourly is resting, or the move is stretched.

7. Every setup explained

Squeeze BreakBollinger Bands move inside the Keltner Channel (a "squeeze": price is coiling quietly). Weekly momentum is up, Daily momentum is below zero and turning up. Ready when it turns; the entry is the 5-day high.
Swing PullbackDaily is in Impulse; the Hourly pulls back 20 to 75% of its last up-leg on lighter volume, then turns up. A 3 to 5 day trade.
Trend PullbackWeekly in Impulse; the Daily pulls back 20 to 75% of its up-leg (the leg must be at least 3 ATR) on drying volume, then turns up.
Overspill (Hourly / Daily / Weekly)In an uptrend, price "spills" back from well above the 20 EMA into the 20 to 50 EMA zone (the value zone), then turns up. Weekly Overspill = positional, Daily = swing, Hourly = short swing.
Range (breakout)A long sideways range with a flat or falling ceiling touched 3+ times. Ready when price is within 1 ATR of the ceiling.
FlagA sharp rise (the pole, 10%+ on Daily) followed by a short, quiet drift that gives back at most a third. Breakout needs 1.5× volume.
RectanglePrice boxed between a flat top and a flat bottom. Ready near the top line.
Wedge / Clean wedgeFalling wedge: two falling lines that narrow. Clean wedge = narrowed to 40% or less of its starting width (tighter, better).
VCPVolatility Contraction Pattern: after a run up, a base whose swings get smaller each time (for example 20%, 10%, 5%) on falling volume. Breakout above the last small swing high.
CupA rounded U-shaped base after an uptrend, often with a small handle. Breakout above the cup's rim.
Breakout (Equity)A tight base in a mid/smallcap, with relative strength 80+; Ready when price is close to the breakout level.
VK tabAll stocks grouped by their VK reading (Buy now, Almost ready, Coming close, No signal and the bearish versions), with the VK advice line.

8. Impulse table and the trend states

Scanner → Impulse table: all F&O stocks with their Monthly, Weekly and Daily state in one table. Filter each timeframe by Impulse or Corrective, and Corrective by its phase.

States come from the momentum line (TTM squeeze momentum: how far price is from its recent average, smoothed) and the EMAs:

StateShortRuleMeaning
ImpulseImpMomentum above zero and rising, price above 20 EMA, 20 EMA above 50 EMA and risingA clean, strong up-move
BullishBullMomentum above zero and rising, but EMAs not fully lined upUp, but not yet a clean impulse; or a pause ending
ToppingTopMomentum above zero but fallingStill up, catching its breath (a shallow pause)
BottomingBottomMomentum below zero but risingDown-move losing force; may turn up
BearishBearMomentum below zero and fallingFalling

Corrective = anything that is not Impulse. M / W / D / H = Monthly / Weekly / Daily / Hourly.

Best way to use it. Tap "M + W Impulse, Daily Topping": the big trend is up and the stock is resting. Star them, then buy when the Daily flips back to Impulse and a setup appears. "All three Impulse" stocks are strong but usually stretched: hold, don't chase. If a stock you hold turns Weekly Corrective, tighten your stop.

9. Index pages

Index → Nifty 50 · Bank Nifty · Fin Nifty · Midcap · Nifty Next 50. Each page shows the chart, M/W/D/H states, the VK reading, futures OI side, PCR, max pain, option-chain support and resistance (strikes with the biggest put and call OI) and relative strength against Nifty.

10. Market pages

Sectors

Each sector index is compared with Nifty (a relative rotation view):

  • Leading: stronger than Nifty and gaining. Best place to buy.
  • Improving: weaker than Nifty but gaining. Next leaders often come from here.
  • Weakening: stronger than Nifty but losing steam.
  • Lagging: weaker and losing. Avoid longs.

Strongest

Every F&O stock (and Nifty 50) ranked by a 0 to 100 strength score: 3-month and 1-month return against Nifty (relative strength), distance from the 52-week high, and trend. The chips under each stock say why it scores high.

Unusual

Spots what is not normal for that stock in the last 1 to 3 days, measured against its own history: volume spikes (hourly volume compared with the same hour on other days), wide-range days, gaps that held, unusual OI jumps and high delivery. Each stock gets a score (today counts fully, yesterday 0.7, two days ago 0.5) and a bias: Buying, Selling or Mixed.

FII footprint

  • Participant OI: how much of the market's index and stock futures FIIs hold long versus short (from NSE's daily participant file). Above 50% long = FIIs net bullish.
  • FII deals: bulk/block deals by foreign institutions (high-frequency traders removed).
  • Accumulation / Distribution: stocks where futures OI and price rose together over 5 to 10 sessions with higher delivery and turnover: the footprint big players usually leave.

Deals and Stars

Deals: NSE bulk and block deals, tagged Mutual fund, Star investor, Insurance or Other, with 30-day net mutual-fund buying per stock. Stars: which well-known investors hold 1% or more of a company, quarter by quarter, from the shareholding filings.

Search

Type any NSE symbol to see everything the site knows about it on one page: setups, states, strength, OI, sector, deals.

🔥 Most scanned (scan hits)

Scanner → 🔥 Most scanned. Counts how many of 15 scans each stock is in today, 1 point each. The 🔥 badge next to a stock name shows the count (3 or more); tap it to see which scans.

  • Early signals (before the move): setup Ready/Triggered, squeeze coiling (Prime Setup), Chup-chap kharidari, RS lead, squeeze + cheap vol, VK Almost ready, FII footprint buying, fund/star buying in Deals.
  • Strength signals (already moving): Monthly + Weekly Impulse, Strongest top 40, VK Buy now, Unusual buying, OI long build-up, sector Leading/Improving, Top 5 positional.
  • Badge colour: purple 3–4, orange 5–6, red 7+. Filters: 3+, 5+, 7+, "2+ early signals", "Good entry zone only".
  • Read it with the chase meter: many scans + 🟢 Good entry zone is the best mix; many scans + 🔴 Don't chase means wait for a dip. Every evening's 3+ list is logged to test whether more scans really means bigger moves.

VK Experimentals (new scanners under test)

Menu → VK Experimentals (on phone: Scanner → 🧪 VK Experimentals). Four new scanners, run every evening at 8:30 pm. Their picks are logged so we can measure them before trusting them.

  • Chup-chap kharidari: price flat while delivery value is 2×+ normal and delivery % is 55+. Quiet buying by big money.
  • RS lead: the stock ÷ Nifty line at a 52-week high while the price is still under its own high. Leaders before they break out.
  • Squeeze + cheap vol: daily squeeze on for 5+ days with volatility in the bottom 25% of its year. Cheap options, coiled price. (IV rank switches on after 40 days of IV history.)
  • Expiry short squeeze: shorts built earlier in the series, price now rising with near-month OI closing and the futures premium improving. Only in the last 5 sessions before monthly expiry.

13. Common questions

Why does a chart show yesterday's candle during the day?

Setups are found on completed candles only, so they don't flicker. Prices, index cards, movers and the heatmap are overlaid live (every 15 minutes, a few minutes late) and marked live.

Why don't I see Buy above / Stop / Target?

Trade levels are shown only to accounts with full access. Everyone sees the same setups, stages, grades and states.

A stock is Ready. Should I buy?

Ready means "plan it", not "buy now". Check the market weather, the sector stage, walls overhead and the chart. Enter only when price confirms, with a stop.

Why is a stock missing from the Impulse table or Strongest?

New listings may not have enough monthly history ("No data"). The F&O list follows NSE; renamed or demerged stocks use their new NSE symbol.

The app looks old after an update.

Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app.
