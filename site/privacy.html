Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google data.

What we keep

  • Your name, email and photo, so you stay signed in and the admin knows who uses the site.
  • When you signed in and visited, the approximate city and country (from your internet connection) and the kind of device and browser.

Sign-in records are kept for up to 120 days. Your profile record is kept while you use the site.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not show ads or use tracking cookies. The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare, which runs the website.

Deleting your data

Ask the admin and your record is deleted.

Questions: write to the email shown on the Google sign-in screen for TradeReady.
